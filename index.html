Our story

From the loom to the modern wardrobe.

Taathaal is an Assam-based craft label built around a simple idea: traditional textiles deserve to be worn, used and reinterpreted—not placed behind glass.

Our edit brings together Mekhela Sador, Assamese-inspired sarees, Gamusa and contemporary textile accessories such as clutches. Each piece is presented with respect for the visual language of the region and an eye toward modern styling.

“Craft changes when people carry it forward.”

“Every thread carries a place, a person, and a story.”
